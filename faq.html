FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What certificates do your products have?
A: Our products are certified with CE, RoHS, REACH and FCC. For built-in lithium batteries, we provide complete documents including MSDS, UN38.8 and full transportation reports to meet global shipping requirements.
Q: Can you customize products? What is the MOQ?
A: Yes, we fully support OEM and ODM services. Our minimum order quantity is 1000 pieces.
Q: Can you provide samples? How long does sampling take?
A: Samples are available. Standard samples can be ready within 3 working days, while customized samples take 10 to 15 days.
Q: What is your production capacity and lead time?
A: Our factory covers over 4000 square meters. We have a daily output of more than 10,000 units, with monthly production capacity reaching 300,000 to 400,000 units. Mass production takes around 30 days. For in-stock regular items, we can arrange shipment within 7 days.
Q: Do you accept OEM & ODM orders?
A: Sure. We have rich experience in OEM and ODM projects to meet your personalized demands.
Q: What is the warranty period for your products?
A: We offer 1 year warranty for all our products.
Q: Do you ship worldwide?
A: Yes, we support worldwide delivery with complete shipping documents.
